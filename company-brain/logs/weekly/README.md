# Weekly Logs

Weekly logs summarize progress, decisions, blockers, and next priorities.

## Suggested Filename

`YYYY-WW.md` or `YYYY-MM-DD-weekly.md`

## Weekly Summary Template

### Week of YYYY-MM-DD

#### Highlights

- What mattered this week?

#### Completed

- What was finished?

#### Active Work

- What continues next week?

#### Decisions

- Link to decision log entries.

#### Risks / Blockers

- What needs attention?

#### Next Week

- What should happen next?

## Agent Guidance

- Use weekly logs for summaries, not detailed task management.
- Make sure active priorities are also reflected in dashboards.
